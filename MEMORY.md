## Decisions
- Deliverable is a website: a gamified guide to choosing from the 38 Bach flower essences, based on the healingherbs.co.uk guide and on people's symptoms.
- Audience: complete beginners. Explain the twelve healers, seven helpers and second nineteen simply.
- Must be engaging to play while teaching.
- PRD drafted (PRD.md, v0.1): beginner-first intro to the three groups, feelings-based matching engine, mini-games (match, sort, scenarios), XP/badges, disclaimers and crisis safeguard.
- PRD group lists (Twelve Healers / Seven Helpers / Second Nineteen) come from general Bach knowledge and must be verified against healingherbs.co.uk.

- 2026-11-04: Prototype built: index.html (single file, no dependencies). Has intro to the 3 groups, feelings picker, results with reasons, Match game, Sort game, XP/levels, card collection and group badges (localStorage), crisis support message, disclaimers. Smoke-tested in the browser pane with no console errors.
- Prototype limits: 1-2 feeling statements per remedy, equal weights; remedy content from general knowledge, NOT yet verified against healingherbs.co.uk; no art, no share/print, no email capture.

- 2026-11-04: VERIFIED group lists against healingherbs.co.uk. Corrections made in index.html and PRD.md: Cerato is a Healer (not Helper); Vine is a Helper (not Healer); blend cap is 5 essences (not 7). Holly now includes hatred/anger. Source also offers a second method, the "7 Emotional Groups" (Fear, Uncertainty, Insufficient interest in present circumstances, Loneliness, Over-sensitivity, Despondency, Over-care for others), which could become an alternative mode.
- Source facts: Healers = personality types, most common starting point; Helpers = long-term states that obscure true nature; Second Nineteen = responses to traumatic events/immediate issues. Dosing per source: 2 drops of each essence in about 20ml water bottle, 4 drops 4 times daily; "5 Flower" counts as one essence. Individual remedy pages, FAQ and the Essence Selector were not read.
- Still open: practitioner review of descriptions; permission to use the source content.

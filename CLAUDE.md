## Project instruction
Make me a website that acts as a gamified guide to choosing the 38 flower essences, based on the guide given by healingherbs.co.uk and based on people's symptoms.

- It should be explained simply so that people understand how to use the twelve healers, seven helpers and second nineteen.
- It should be engaging to play, while imparting some information to people who are completely new to Bach flower remedies.
- Create a product requirement document (PRD) for this project.

## Working rules
- Keep this folder as the project root. Put all files here, including the PRD.
- Keep remedy content (names, emotional themes, keywords, group) in one data file, separate from the game logic.
- Educational framing only. Do not make medical claims. Bach flower essences are complementary and do not diagnose, treat or cure illness.
- Use healingherbs.co.uk as the content source. Do not copy its text verbatim; paraphrase and credit the source.
